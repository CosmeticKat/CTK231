Video Production Portfolio

Welcome over to the Video Production area! This is work all centered around video editing, filming, and design! Below are all of my current works that I have done either for myself, or for others in work!

Youtube

My own Youtube channel I started creating content near the end of 2022 with my origonal friend group in VRChat, with the main focus being - VRChat content. More specifically funny moments of our adventures and times hanging out, taking inspiration from other creators like MullyVR and JoshDub. After years have passed, I started streaming and doing lets play of Deltarune chapter 3 and 4 during the summer of 2025, with burnout coming soon afterwords, and I now focus more on story time VR animation. Through the years I learned how to edit and splice hour long content along with adding sound effects, background audio, and other visual ques to keep engagement. Along the way I also found success in short form content to promote the long form content that I already had, and now with new direction, i have found new fun and exciting ways to make even better content!

Graviteh Editor

Graviteh is a hockey content creator over on Youtube. After a long time following, Graviteh grew into the channel he is now, before posting a video about channel updates and the fact he was looking for Video editors. After applying, I joined his team in October of 2025. Ive learned alot about the type of content that is created: B roll footage with main audio and a mix of the origonal content.
